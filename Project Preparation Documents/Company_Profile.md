# Company Profile - Gamer's Zone

**Business:** Retail of gaming products in Egypt.

**Products:**
- Consoles: PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Xbox
- Controllers for each platform
- Online subscriptions: PS Plus and Xbox Game Pass
- Bundles: console + controller + games

**Sales channels:** Store (Point of Sale) and online orders.

**Promotions:** White Friday and Christmas offers through pricelists.

**Currency:** EGP | **Tax:** 14% VAT

**Departments:** Sales, Purchase, Warehouse, Accounting

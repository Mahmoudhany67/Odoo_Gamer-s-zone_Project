# Basic Configuration of the Odoo Database - Gamer's Zone

1. Company profile: name, logo, address, tax ID, currency EGP, language, timezone
2. Apps installed: CRM, Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Accounting, Point of Sale, Manufacturing, Approvals, Employees
3. Departments and users created with access rights per module
4. Accounting: Egypt fiscal localization, chart of accounts, VAT 14%, journals (Cash, Cards), payment terms
5. Inventory: warehouses, locations, routes (Buy, MTO), putaway rules, reordering rules
6. Products: consoles, controllers, subscriptions, bundles with variants and prices
7. Pricelists: White Friday and Christmas
8. Point of Sale: Retail store with cash and card payment methods
9. Opening balances: trial balance, partner balances, stock quantities

Screenshots of the configured settings: see `Basic_Database_Configuration_Settings.pdf` in this folder.
